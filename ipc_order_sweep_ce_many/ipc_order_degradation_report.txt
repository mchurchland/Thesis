Why even orders are near zero here:
The drive is zero-mean and approximately symmetric on [-1, 1], and tanh dynamics are odd.
That makes reservoir-state statistics mostly odd-symmetric, so even Legendre targets (P2, P4, ...) have weak correlation with the readout features.
Because IPC uses clipped R^2, these weak fits usually collapse to values near zero.
Small nonzero even-order values can still appear from finite-sample noise and imperfect symmetry.
